THE TWINKLE STANDARD

Our Rigorous
5-Stage Restoration Process

True detailing is science, chemistry, and craftsmanship combined. We follow a strict protocol designed to protect and elevate every micron of your finish.

01

Chemical Decon

pH-neutral high lubricity snow foam wash, non-acidic fallout dissolver, tar extraction, and synthetic clay bar surface cleansing.

02

Digital Mapping

Ultrasonic paint depth gauge readings across all panels. High-CRI LED inspection to diagnose swirl depth, marring, and thin areas.

03

Precision Correction

Multi-stage micro-abrasive compounding with rotary and dual-action polishers to remove scratches without clear coat compromise.

04

Alcohol Sterilization

Full surface wipe-down with pure isopropyl alcohol blend to strip residual polishing oils, guaranteeing optimal ceramic covalent bonding.

05

Ceramic / PPF Curing

Pure silica glass coating or self-healing PPF application followed by short-wave infrared heat lamps for ultimate cross-linked hardness.
